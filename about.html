Background

Pablo Rodriguez founded Hawthorne & Fletcher with the conviction that independent equity research had become an endangered species. Having spent years watching the sell-side bend analysis to serve deal flow rather than investors, the goal was simple: build something different.

With a background spanning fundamental equity analysis, financial modelling, and sector coverage across European and global markets, the focus at H&F is on work that endures beyond the next earnings call — research that asks whether a business is genuinely good, at what price, and why.

Approach to research

Every report published under the Hawthorne & Fletcher name starts from the same place: what does this business actually do, and how does it earn money? The analysis builds from first principles rather than consensus models.

Investment philosophy
01
Independence above all
Research funded by clients, not by companies we cover. No banking relationships. No rating inflation. The conclusions go where the evidence leads.
02
First principles over consensus
Consensus models are a starting point, not a destination. The interesting questions are always about what the market is getting wrong — and why.
03
Patience as a strategy
The best ideas require time to play out. Research is written with a multi-year horizon — not to fit a quarterly publication schedule.
04
Transparency of reasoning
Every recommendation makes the reasoning explicit. Readers should be able to disagree with a specific assumption — not just accept or reject a conclusion.
01
Conflict-free
No underwriting. No advisory fees from covered companies. Research funded entirely by readers and clients.
02
Fundamental
Bottom-up analysis from the company level up. Not macro-driven. Not momentum-driven.
03
Long-horizon
Written for investors who think in years. No target prices anchored to the next quarter.
04
Transparent
Assumptions, models, and reasoning are all visible. Agree or disagree — but always know why.
