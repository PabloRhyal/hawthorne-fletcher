What We Do

Research-driven.
Independently minded.

We produce rigorous, conflict-free equity research for individual investors, family offices, and institutional clients who demand analysis untethered from investment banking relationships.

01

Equity Research Reports

Deep-dive fundamental analysis on individual companies — valuation, competitive positioning, risk factors, and actionable conclusions.

02

Sector Coverage

Ongoing thematic coverage across selected sectors, tracking industry dynamics, regulatory shifts, and relative value opportunities.

03

Portfolio Review

Independent audit of existing equity holdings — identifying concentration risk, valuation mismatches, and strategic alignment gaps.

04

Investment Due Diligence

Pre-investment analysis for private and public securities, stress-testing assumptions and surfacing the questions that matter most.

05

Bespoke Analysis

Custom research mandates for clients with specific informational needs — built around your questions, not our product catalogue.

06

Advisory Retainer

Ongoing analytical support for investment committees and family offices — structured as a monthly retainer with defined deliverables.

About

Analysis without agenda.

Hawthorne & Fletcher was founded on a single conviction: that quality equity research should serve investors, not underwriters. Our work is funded entirely by our clients — not by the companies we cover.

We combine rigorous financial modelling with qualitative judgement built over years of following markets. Our reports are written to be read, understood, and acted upon — not filed away.

Based in the United Kingdom, we work with clients across Europe and internationally who value research that tells them what they need to hear, not what they want to hear.
