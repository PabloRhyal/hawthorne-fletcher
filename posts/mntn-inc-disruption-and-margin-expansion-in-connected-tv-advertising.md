## Executive Summary

The secular tailwinds behind Connected TV (CTV) are no longer a forward-looking projection; they are an active reallocation of corporate capital. In 2026, global CTV ad spend is projected to hit $37.95 billion (a 14.5% YoY growth), with 70% of digital advertisers planning to increase their streaming TV budgets to combat the fragmentation of traditional media.

At the epicenter of this migration sits MNTN Inc. ($MNTN), a platform consistently mispriced by generalist market participants as a cyclical marketing agency. By analyzing MNTN’s recently released Full Year 2025 audited financials and its Q1 2026 earnings, we outline how a pure software-driven architecture is triggering a massive EBITDA inflection point.

## The Business Model: Deconstructing the Performance TV Paradigm

To understand MNTN’s structural advantage, one must first understand the fundamental fragmentation of the Connected TV (CTV) and programmatic advertising supply chain. Historically, advertising on television required navigating a labyrinth of media buyers, upfront presentations, and opaque agency margins. Even with the rise of programmatic platforms (such as Demand-Side Platforms or DSPs like The Trade Desk), buying CTV ad space remained separate from data attribution. Advertisers bought impressions, not outcomes.

MNTN operates an entirely different architecture: a self-serve, software-first platform specifically engineered to treat television screens as a performance marketing channel.

#### The Monetization Mechanics: Beyond the Ad Agency Model

Unlike traditional agencies that charge a legacy percentage-of-ad-spend fee (which naturally aligns the agency’s incentives with spending more capital rather than maximizing efficiency), MNTN functions as a proprietary software ecosystem.

The company eliminates intermediate friction by integrating premium streaming inventory management directly inside its platform. Advertisers commit their budgets directly through the software, which uses automated machine learning algorithms to buy, optimize, and rotate placements in real-time.

By eliminating the hidden markups and margins typical of traditional media-buying intermediaries, MNTN reallocates that lost capital back into the advertiser’s campaign efficiency. This creates a compelling economic proposition: higher software utility translates directly into lower customer acquisition costs (CAC) for the brand.

#### Structural Moats: Verified Visits™ and Creative Automation

A critical friction point in digital advertising is attribution fraud and “fat-finger” mobile metrics. On a television screen, you cannot click an ad. This makes tracking conversions notoriously difficult. MNTN solves this through its proprietary Verified Visits™ attribution engine. Instead of relying on crude post-view windows or imprecise IP matching, the platform tracks when a user sees a CTV ad on their television and subsequently visits the advertiser’s website on another device (such as a laptop or smartphone) within a strictly defined, fraud-mitigated timeframe.

Furthermore, MNTN solved the primary bottleneck holding mid-market brands back from TV advertising: the prohibitive cost of high-quality video production. Through strategic vertical integration—specifically its acquisition of QuickFrame AI—the company offers automated, scalable video ad creation directly integrated into the software. The recent release of QuickFrame AI 3.0 allows brands to deploy TV-ready commercials in minutes, systematically lowering the barrier to entry and allowing agile digital brands to test ad creatives with the same velocity they use on Meta or Google.

#### Supply-Side Alignment and Scale

By building direct relationships with premium publishers and major streaming networks (leveraging internal expertise from traditional network legacy players like NBCUniversal), MNTN bypasses traditional supply-side aggregators.

For investors, this self-serve software model unlocks massive operating leverage. The cost to serve an incremental dollar of ad spend through an automated software platform is nearly zero. As mid-market and enterprise advertisers migrate their budgets from decaying linear television over to CTV, MNTN’s revenue scales exponentially while its operational cost base remains relatively flat. This decoupling of revenue from headcount is the exact mechanism that will drive aggressive EBITDA margin expansion over the coming cycles.

## Financial Mechanics: The Operating Leverage Engine

The core of our investment thesis does not rely on speculative multiple expansion; it is anchored in the mathematical certainty of operating leverage. In software-driven advertising platforms, the cost hierarchy behaves fundamentally differently than in traditional service-oriented firms. Once the core code, bidding algorithms, and direct supply-side publisher integrations are fully capitalized, the marginal cost of routing an additional dollar of advertising spend through the platform approaches zero.

### Scale, TAM, and Revenue Trajectory

MNTN’s historical financials demonstrate an aggressive top-line growth vector. For the Full Year 2025, MNTN reported annual revenue of $290.09 million, representing a robust 28.6% YoY growth compared to $225.57 million in fiscal year 2024.

This momentum has carried heavily into 2026. In its latest Q1 2026 earnings report, MNTN generated $73.67 million in revenue, beating consensus estimates and marking a 14.2% YoY increase. This growth was primarily driven by a 26% increase in active Performance TV (PTV) customers, proving that the platform is successfully onboarding mid-market enterprise accounts.

### Gross Margin Profile and Capital Efficiency

A major misunderstanding by generalist market participants is categorizing MNTN alongside low-margin media agencies. Agencies scale linearly; to handle double the ad budget, they require roughly double the account managers and media buyers, capping their operational efficiency.

MNTN's software-first architecture flips this dynamic on its head. Following the strategic divestiture of its non-core creative services arm (*Maximum Effort*) in Q2 2025, MNTN's gross margins have expanded dramatically:

* Q4 2025 Gross Margin: Improved to 82.0% (up from 77.0% in Q4 2024).
* Q1 2026 Gross Margin: Sustained at 81.5%.

This high-margin profile allows MNTN to aggressively reinvest in its engineering stack—R&D and engineering headcount grew 23% in Q1 2026 to optimize automated targeting—while keeping the balance sheet pristine with $210 million in cash and equivalents, and zero debt outstanding.

### The EBITDA Inflection Point

Operating leverage is a double-edged sword, but when a platform crosses its cash-flow break-even threshold during an industry secular expansion, the results are explosive.

In 2024, Adjusted EBITDA stood at $38.8 million (17.0% margin). By the end of 2025, Adjusted EBITDA grew to $68.0 million, representing 23.0% of revenue.

The inflection is fully visible in the Q1 2026 results, where the Adjusted EBITDA margin hit 22.2%. Management’s full-year 2026 guidance calls for Adjusted EBITDA between $94.6 million and $99.6 million.

As top-line revenue scales toward the $350M midpoint for 2026, fixed operating expenses (OpEx) are growing at a significantly slower rate. Every incremental dollar of gross profit generated from platform ad spend is dropping straight to the operating income line, transforming MNTN from a cash-burning tech IPO into a highly efficient free-cash-flow machine.

## Competitive Landscape & Structural Risks

A high-conviction thesis is incomplete without a rigorous stress-test of its vulnerabilities. While MNTN’s software architecture is fundamentally superior to legacy agency models, the company operates within an aggressive, rapidly consolidating AdTech ecosystem. Investors must carefully monitor two primary structural risks that could disrupt the platform’s long-term margin trajectory.

### The Rise of Walled Gardens (Inventory Isolation)

The most significant threat to independent Demand-Side Platforms (DSPs) and optimization software like MNTN is the fragmentation of streaming inventory. Mega-cap media networks and streaming platforms are increasingly building their own closed ad-tech ecosystems.

* The Risk: Tech giants with massive proprietary scale (such as Amazon Prime Video, Netflix, and Disney/Hulu) have the financial capacity to build in-house self-serve ad platforms. If these dominant content owners decide to restrict third-party software access to their premium ad inventory in order to capture 100% of the media margins, MNTN’s available inventory pool could contract, forcing mid-market advertisers to diversify their ad spend across multiple platform-native dashboards.
* The Counter-Thesis: Most mid-market brands do not have the operational capacity or headcount to manage 4.4 different CTV partners simultaneously (the current 2026 industry average). MNTN’s value proposition relies on being a unified, aggregated terminal that deduplicates reach and manages frequency across the entire open internet and CTV spectrum.

### Execution Risk in AI and Attribution Modeling

MNTN’s competitive moat is heavily reliant on its proprietary machine learning bidding engine and its *Verified Visits™* attribution tracking.

* The Risk: AdTech is a continuous arms race. Competitors with massive data scale, such as The Trade Desk (TTD) or specialized CTV supply-side platforms, are constantly upgrading their programmatic capabilities. If MNTN fails to aggressively maintain its algorithmic edge, or if updates to cross-device privacy regulations (e.g., changes in IP tracking or device graph identifiers) degrade the precision of its attribution models, its core value proposition (guaranteeing measurable ROAS) could suffer from performance degradation. Continuous R&D reinvestment is not optional; it is a vital operational requirement to protect the *moat*.

## Valuation & Concluding Thoughts: The Asymmetry of Convective Growth

When evaluating MNTN, the market consistently commits a category error by pricing it as a cyclical marketing agency rather than a scalable infrastructure layer for Connected TV. This mispricing creates a highly compelling asymmetry for long-term fundamental investors.

Our conviction is rooted in the structural inevitability of capital migration. Linear television ad spend is a melting ice cube; that capital has to go somewhere, and it will naturally flow toward platforms that can prove conversion metrics and guarantee a strict Return on Ad Spend (ROAS). MNTN’s self-serve software and proprietary attribution engine are uniquely positioned to absorb this immense tidal wave of enterprise capital.

### The Verdict

While near-term volatility, sales-force investments (53 new sales hires onboarding in 2026), and the competitive positioning of major “walled gardens” warrant close, continuous monitoring, MNTN’s financial architecture is built for extreme scalability. Turning GAAP profitable in Q1 2026 with a net income of $8.76 million (compared to a loss of $21.11 million in Q1 2025) proves the inflection is here. As revenue scales past $350M this year, the controlled fixed-cost base will trigger an intense EBITDA and free-cash-flow expansion. For *Hawthorne & Fletcher Research*, MNTN represents a premier technology asset disguised as an advertising tool, offering an exceptional margin of safety and immense structural upside for patient capital.
